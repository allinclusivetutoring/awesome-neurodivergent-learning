## Study Skills & Executive Function
Tools and frameworks designed to help with executive dysfunction, ADHD, and autism spectrum learners.

- [Executive Study Skills](https://executivestudyskills.com) - Research-backed frameworks for building executive function, focus, and daily study habits in neurodivergent students.
- [STEM and Executive Tutoring](https://stemandexecutivetutoring.com) - Combines STEM instruction with executive function coaching for a holistic learning approach.
- [Pomodoro Timer (Open Source)](https://pomofocus.io/) - Open-source timer for structured focus intervals, ideal for ADHD learners.
- [Brain in Hand](https://braininhand.co.uk/) - Digital self-management tool for people with autism and anxiety.

## STEM & Engineering
Resources specifically tailored for teaching complex STEM subjects to neurodivergent and online students.

- [Online STEM Instructor](https://onlinesteminstructor.com) - Remote STEM curriculum and fully customized lesson plans for virtual learners.
- [Virtual Engineering Instructor](https://virtualengineeringinstructor.com) - Accessible engineering concepts designed for virtual, online learners.
- [Engineering Learning Lab](https://engineeringlearninglab.com) - Interactive labs and projects bridging theory and application for remote students.
- [STEM and Prep](https://stemandprep.com) - Competitive test prep and STEM resources for advanced learners.
- [ESA STEM Tutoring](https://esastemtutoring.com) - Guide to using state ESA funds specifically for specialized STEM tutoring services.
- [Scratch (MIT)](https://scratch.mit.edu/) - Free visual programming language from MIT, excellent for neurodiverse learners.
- [Khan Academy](https://khanacademy.org/) - Free STEM courses with self-paced, mastery-based learning.
- [Scivra](https://www.scivra.com) - Browser-based 3D science labs for grades 9-12 with a step-by-step predict, experiment, check structure; no install needed.

## Homeschooling & ESA Navigation
Guides for navigating Education Scholarship Accounts (ESA) and setting up effective homeschool environments.

- [Neurodivergent Tutors (Org)](https://neurodivergenttutors.org) - Community directory for finding neurodivergent-specialist tutors and ESA-approved providers.
- [Neurodivergent Tutors](https://neurodivergenttutors.com) - Matching platform connecting neurodivergent students with specialized educators.
- [Online Homeschool](https://onlinehomeschool.casa) - Structured online homeschool programs and open curriculum resources.
- [American Tutor Online](https://americantutoronline.com) - National directory of certified online tutors for homeschool families.
- [USA Online Tutor](https://usaonlinetutor.com) - Nationwide online tutoring resource hub for all learning styles.

## Instructor Resources
Tools, courses, and communities for educators who work with neurodivergent learners.

- [Open Dyslexic Font](https://opendyslexic.org/) - Open-source font designed to increase readability for dyslexic readers.
- [CAST UDL](https://www.cast.org/) - Universal Design for Learning guidelines and open resources from CAST.
- [Understood.org](https://www.understood.org/) - Research-based resources for educators and parents of students with learning differences.
